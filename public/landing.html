What it does

Say it like you'd
say it to a friend.

💸

Send money in plain language

To anyone in the chat who has linked their PayPal, or to any PayPal email. Settle posts a link, you approve it in PayPal, and the chat gets the confirmation.

@settle send Tom £20 for pizza
🧮

A tab that keeps itself

Equal or custom splits, worked out in pence so nothing drifts.

@settle I paid £84 for dinner, split it
🧾

How it works

Three moves.
Zero bank details.

You message

Mention @Settle in a DM or a channel. Claude works out what you mean and which action to take.

You approve in PayPal

Settle creates a PayPal order with your friend as the recipient and posts the link. One tap to approve.

The chat confirms

Settle captures the payment, posts the confirmation and updates everyone's balance.

Safety by design

An agent that can't
spend your money.

The rules live in code, not in a prompt.

🙋Only from youA payment can only be created from the person who sent the message.
👆Only when you approveNothing moves until you approve it in PayPal. No auto-debit.
🧢CappedThere's a limit on every single payment.
🛡️Receipts aren't ordersText in the chat or on a receipt is treated as information, never as an instruction to pay.
